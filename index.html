CASE EVIDENCE RECORDED
CASE #05

The Broken Coaster

At 21:40, after the park had closed, a launched coaster train was sent round on a routine test cycle. It cleared the launch track, climbed into the vertical loop, slowed, and stalled before the crest. It rolled back down and the block brakes caught it. Nobody was aboard.

The park says the launch system under-delivered and the train never had enough energy to begin with. The ride manufacturer says the launch was fine and something on the train was dragging.

Both stories end with a train stalled on the loop. They differ in where the energy went.

Your job is an energy audit. Work out how much energy the train started with, how much it should have lost to normal resistance, and how much actually disappeared. The difference decides which account the evidence supports.
